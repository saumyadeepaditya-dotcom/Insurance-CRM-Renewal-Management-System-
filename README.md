# Insurance CRM – Renewal Management System

**Business Analysis Portfolio Project | Insurance Domain**

## Project Overview

An independent Business Analysis portfolio case study focused on designing a centralized CRM solution to improve insurance policy renewal management.

## Business Problem

Manual renewal tracking, fragmented customer information and inconsistent follow-ups can lead to missed renewal opportunities, delayed customer engagement and policy lapses.

## Project Objectives

- Improve policy renewal tracking.
- Support timely renewal alerts.
- Centralize customer and policy information.
- Improve follow-up and renewal outcome tracking.
- Provide management visibility through Power BI dashboards.

## Dashboard

The project includes two Power BI pages:

1. **Renewal Management Overview** – Management-level view of renewal performance and key indicators.
2. **Renewal Operations** – Operational view of upcoming renewals, renewal trends and follow-up status.

The proposed CRM supports operational renewal management, while Power BI provides management reporting and analytical visibility.

## Dataset

A synthetic dataset containing **4,000 policy records** was created for portfolio and demonstration purposes. It does not contain actual customer or proprietary company data.

## Project Scope and Limitations

This project focuses on the business analysis and proposed design of a centralized insurance renewal management solution.

The Power BI dashboard is a reporting prototype built using synthetic data. The CRM solution has not been developed or deployed, and no live system integrations are included.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio case study inspired by insurance-domain experience. It does not represent a deployed CRM solution or proprietary company documentation.
