# Insurance-CRM-Renewal-Management-System
Business Analysis Portfolio Project – Insurance CRM Renewal Management System

## Project Overview
A Business Analysis portfolio case study focused on
designing a centralized CRM solution to improve
insurance policy renewal management.

## Business Problem
Manual renewal tracking, fragmented customer
information and inconsistent follow-ups can lead
to missed renewal opportunities and policy lapses.

## Project Objectives
- Improve policy renewal tracking.
- Support timely renewal alerts.
- Centralize customer and policy information.
- Improve follow-up and renewal outcome tracking.
- Provide management visibility through Power BI.
